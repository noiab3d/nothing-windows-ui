# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Installer is now in English.

## 1.1.0

- The app follows your Windows language, with a language picker (English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian).
- The title shows the model reported by the earbuds themselves.
- Controls adapt to the detected model, with a warning on untested ones.
- New: custom EQ, dual connection, audio codec, Disconnect / Connect buttons.
- Prompt to open the Bluetooth settings when Bluetooth is off.
- The panel slides in and out from the bottom of the screen; a checker dissolve plays on language and screen changes.
- Dot-matrix artwork in the installer.

## 1.0.0

- First version: tray app with battery, noise control, EQ, Bass Enhance, gestures, in-ear detection, low latency, find my earbuds and fit test.
